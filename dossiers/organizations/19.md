# Directorate MS (SVR-MS)


## Summary

MS stands for measures of support (meropriyatiya sodeistviya), the new term for active measures. This directorate 8formerly known as directorate A) is responsible for election meddling, disinformation campaigns, and other operations related to the realm of active measures.
