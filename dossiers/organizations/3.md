# Main Intelligence Directorate (GRU)

## Summary

GRU stands for main intelligence directorate (glavnoe razvedyvatel'noye upravlyeniye), and it is the heir organization of the soviet GRU. Because it is nowadays called "main directorate" of the general staff of the armed forces of the Russian federation, the contemporary official acronym is GU (glavnoe upravlyeniye).

The GRU is the Russian military intelligence service (analogous to the Amrican DIA but with enhanced scope in fields like SIGINT and PsyOps).
