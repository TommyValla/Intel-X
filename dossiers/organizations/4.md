# Foreign Intelligence Service (SVR)

## Summary

Heir to the fist chief diretorate of the Soviet-era KGB, with its operatives still calling themselves pe-ge-u-chniki. Its operational core contains services and directorates responsible for different lines of work (like counterintelligence for Directorate K or economic intelligence for Service ER), as well as eleven geographical departments supervising SVR employees assigned to residences abroad.
