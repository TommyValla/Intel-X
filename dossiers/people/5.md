# Igor Kostyukov

Igor Olegovich Kostyukov is the head of the **GRU** (GU), a position he was promoted to in 2018 after the death of the previous chief military spy Igor Korobov. Such death came soon after the assassination of **Sergei Skripal** and his daughter Yulia in Salisbury (March 2018), when Kostyukov was serving as the GRU's **first deputy head**. Korobov himself had been appointed after the sudden death of his predecessor, Igor Sergun.

## Biography

Igor Kostyukov was born in 1961 in Amur Oblast, Russian SFSR, Soviet Union. His education included a naval school and the Military Diplomatic Academy of the Soviet Armed Forces 

## Career

After his studies, Kostyukov was reportedly posted in Switzerlad and Italy. Kostyukov’s son Oleg once worked at the Russian Embassy in Italy. 

### Syria
Kostyukov was directly involved in leading the Russian military operation in Syria. He was directly involved in guiding intelligence and special operations against Islamic State (ISIS) targets and Syrian rebel factions. This role required him to make frequent, highly classified trips to Damascus to coordinate directly with Syrian authorities. For this, Vladimir Putin awarded him the title Hero of Russia in 2017. 

In January 2020, he was responsible for ensuring Putin's security during his trip to Syria. After Assad's fall, Kostyukov kept his relevance in bilateral relations with Syria, leading talks with Al-Shahara.

### Diplomatic talks with Ukraine

On May 14, 2025, Putin signed an order regarding the composition of the Russian delegation for negotiations with Ukraine in Istanbul, including Kostyukov. The delegation was headed by presidential aide Vladimir Medinsky. That evening, Kostyukov took part in a meeting to prepare for negotiations with the Ukrainian side, after which he and other delegates departed for Turkey.

On January 23, 2026, Presidential Aide Yuri Ushakov announced that Igor Kostyukov would lead Russia's security negotiating team at the planned trilateral meeting with the United States and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi.

The third round of talks between Russia, Ukraine, and the United States took place in Geneva on February 17 and 18. The Russian delegation was led by Vladimir Medinsky , aide to the Russian president, and also included Deputy Foreign Minister Mikhail Galuzin and Kostyukov.
